# How I want my cover letters written

The AI reads this guide each time it writes a cover letter. Change it to suit you.

- Under one page: three or four short paragraphs.
- Start with the date, then "Dear Hiring Manager," unless the ad names a person.
- Paragraph 1: the role I'm applying for, and in one sentence why it appeals to me.
- Paragraph 2: two achievements from my master profile that match what the ad asks for most.
- Paragraph 3: how I like to work, and why that suits this team or company.
- Close: I'd welcome a conversation, and how to reach me. Sign off with "Kind regards," and my name.
- Warm and direct. No clichés such as "I am writing to apply for".
- Only facts from my master profile. Never invent numbers, employers or certifications.
- Australian spelling.
